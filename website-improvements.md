# Website improvement notes

## Overall assessment

The site already feels credible, clean, and trustworthy, but it is underselling Adrian's skills and expertise. The main opportunity is to shift the homepage away from feeling like a resume and toward feeling like a sharp positioning page: who Adrian helps, what problems he solves, and what proof supports that claim.

## Core recommendation

Lead with the business value of Adrian's technical range.

Right now, the page mainly communicates: "I've done many things."

It should instead communicate: "I help organizations solve hard data and quant problems end-to-end."

That framing makes the mix of academia, startups, and finance feel like a commercial advantage rather than just career history.

## Key improvements

| Area | Current state | Recommended improvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Name, title, and social icons, but no strong pitch or CTA | Add a sharper headline, short subhead, and 1-2 CTAs |
| Positioning | "Quantitative Developer & Data Engineer" is credible but broad | Reframe around outcomes: research to production, data platforms, quant tools, technical leadership |
| Proof near the top | Strong company logos, but no explanation | Add a short proof strip with logos and a few metrics or credibility signals |
| About section | Strong but generic and text-heavy | Rewrite as a tighter value proposition focused on what clients, collaborators, or employers get |
| Projects | Interesting, but written more as descriptions than evidence | Turn them into short case studies with problem, solution, and why it matters |
| Selected works | Impressive, but long and mixed across different eras | Split into Recent work, Writing, Academic background, and Archive |
| Conversion | No clear next step for visitors who want to work with Adrian | Add clear CTAs such as Work with me, Email me, Read my writing, or See GitHub |
| Visual design | Clean but visually flat | Increase hierarchy, contrast, section definition, and overall polish |

## Hero section recommendations

The hero should do more than introduce Adrian by name. It should quickly explain what he does, why that matters, and what a visitor should do next.

### Suggested direction

- **Headline:** "I build production-grade data, quant, and ML systems."
- **Supporting line:** "12+ years across academia, startups, and finance."
- **CTAs:** "Work with me" and "Read my writing"

This would immediately make the homepage feel more outcome-focused and commercially relevant.

## Messaging and positioning

The current copy is credible, but it is somewhat broad and reflective. It needs to be more pointed and outcome-driven.

### Recommended framing

Position Adrian as someone who can:

- take ideas from research to production
- build reliable data and quant systems
- combine technical depth with practical delivery
- operate across engineering, modeling, and leadership

The page should emphasize not only what Adrian has done, but what kinds of high-value problems he is especially good at solving.

## Proof and credibility

The company logos are strong and should remain, but they need context.

### Recommended addition

Add a short trust/proof strip near the top with:

- selected company logos
- years of experience
- audience or readership size
- open-source adoption or package reach
- publications, talks, or featured media

This will help visitors understand the logos as evidence, rather than just decoration.

## About section improvements

The current About section has good substance, but it can be tightened. It currently reads more like a biography than a concise professional proposition.

### Recommended direction

Reduce it to a short, sharp summary that explains:

- the kinds of problems Adrian solves
- the environments he has solved them in
- the advantage of his mixed background

The goal is to make the section feel like a strong professional narrative, not general background.

## Project section improvements

The Active projects section is one of the site's strongest assets, but it should do more selling.

### Recommended direction

Turn each project card into a mini case study that covers:

1. the problem
2. what Adrian built
3. who it helps
4. why it demonstrates expertise

The current cards are informative, but they can do more to signal practical impact and technical distinction.

## Selected works improvements

The long list of papers, talks, courses, and older projects is impressive, but it currently pulls the page toward Adrian's past rather than his present value.

### Recommended direction

Keep the material, but reorganize it into clearer sections such as:

1. Recent work
2. Writing and speaking
3. Research and publications
4. Archive

This preserves credibility while making the site feel more current and commercially sharp.

## Recommended homepage structure

1. **Hero** - headline, subhead, CTAs, and possibly a headshot or distinctive visual
2. **Trust strip** - company logos plus a few proof points
3. **What I do** - three clear service/capability pillars such as Quant systems, Data engineering, and Applied ML / research translation
4. **Featured work** - strongest case studies with outcomes and context
5. **Writing / speaking / open source** - establish visible thought leadership
6. **Background / publications** - secondary credibility section
7. **Contact CTA** - explicit invitation to get in touch

## Design and aesthetic recommendations

The current design is tasteful and uncluttered, but it feels visually flat and somewhat generic.

### Recommended upgrades

- introduce a stronger visual hierarchy
- use a larger and more distinctive headline style
- reduce the empty feel of the splash section and bring more value above the fold
- add stronger section contrast through spacing, background changes, or dividers
- improve card styling with more depth and better typography
- make project and logo sections feel more curated and intentional
- choose one stronger accent color for a more premium visual identity

The goal is not to make the site busier. It is to make it feel more deliberate, premium, and memorable.

## Conversion improvements

The current site does not make the next step obvious for visitors who may want to hire Adrian, collaborate, or explore his work further.

### Recommended additions

Add repeated, explicit CTA opportunities such as:

- Work with me
- Book a call
- Email me
- Read Open Source Quant
- View GitHub

These should appear in the hero and again near the bottom of the page.

## Strategic takeaway

The site already establishes intelligence and credibility. What it needs now is stronger positioning and stronger conversion.

The best next move is to make the homepage feel less like a personal homepage and more like a professional front door for Adrian's expertise.

## High-priority changes

1. Rewrite the hero and add strong CTAs
2. Add a "What I do" section framed around outcomes
3. Turn projects into case studies with stronger proof
4. Reorganize publications and older work into a secondary credibility section
5. Upgrade typography, spacing, and section structure to feel more premium

## Small quality issues in the current page

- The footer contains a broken closing `<a>` tag for the X link
- The HTML uses custom tags such as `<name>`, `<job>`, `<quote>`, and `<tag>`, which weakens semantics and polish

These are smaller issues, but fixing them would improve the site's overall professionalism.
